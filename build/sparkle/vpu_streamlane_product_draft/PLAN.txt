Proof-boundary gaps and recommended decomposition
-------------------------------------------------
The composite is Mealy, not Moore: st_sel[0] directly depends on in_v/ret_r;
st_a/b/c depend on current operands and later stage st_y. Therefore copying the
SE-tail all-leaf-Moore composition unchanged would be unsound. Two sound options:
A. Recommended: flatten ONLY concrete EXT1 composite into bank parent while
   retaining24 FP boxes. Candidate parent18982bits, no mutable RAM expected.
   All external c_uy/c_qy stay real unrestricted inputs. Actual FP y cones are
   Moore under complete FP relations, so parent all-D/outputs/child-input equality
   and concrete leaf preservation compose noncircularly. Avoid flat FP arithmetic.
   This requires one new complete parent proof; old separate lane/composite
   graphs, source recipes and transitions remain authenticated reference evidence.
B. Reuse separate parents only after proving complete port dependency order for
   both original/native composite and bank. Dependency union must be acyclic;
   st_y stage0 is registered FP result, stages1/2 external tail results. Do not
   assume all composite outputs equal before proving its actual current inputs.
   This option saves SAT work but adds a nontrivial Mealy composition certificate.
