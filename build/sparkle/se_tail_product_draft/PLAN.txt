   all external outputs, and every leaf's en/a/b input. Both pe and cen remain

   check leaf references are actual otpu_fmul(LAT2)/otpu_fadd(LAT4), including
   identity u_pad, and exact gate definitions. No legacy after-fill FP theorem

3. Check y Moore cones in concrete exposed leaf steps: only state/constants,

   the parent theorem is applied. Parent gives equal next inputs; concrete leaf

4. Combine native register/leaf witnesses and shared arbitrary RAM witness for
   base; induction yields initialized synthesized product refinement for89824.
   Generic ProductComposition.lean is supporting logic only, not a concrete
   kernel-checked instantiation of RTL/compiler/SAT certificates.

Four diagnostic bits: attainable independent extension
-------------------------------------------------------
Prefer a separate tiny next-state/initializer theorem over rebuilding or
reproving the82820-bit parent. Do not pretend the old theorem includes dq_n.
Source transition on both sides is reset-or-init priority, then pe:
next dq_n=0 if rst||init, else pe ? dq_n+dv-d_pop (mod16) : dq_n.
Original d_pop=pe&&um.v&&um.j==0; um comes only from warm and ub_q registers;
dv comes from the six-cycle dv pipeline. No raw RAM data or FP combinational
logic is needed in this counter's next-state cone.
