What source inspection supports, and what it does not
The last two observed groups had one complete actual shared operand vector and
one nonidentical vector. Original memory expansion retains defined constant-read
muxes with unused X arms; the conservative existing sharing whitelist does not
recognize those as known. This is a plausible recurring representation pattern,
not evidence about uninspected fullgraph comparators. Existing exact read IDs and
word-Q ownership may help explain candidates after inventory. Do not silently
extend the knownness whitelist, alias unknown-selected branches, or call raw
SatGen unknown-value correlations irrelevant.

selected arm make Y known/equal that arm regardless of the unused arm. A narrowly
reviewed canonical-knownness certificate could therefore expose word-read identity.
This needs explicit rule/domain/provenance and guards; it must not generalize to
undefined selected arms (aliasing would change unknown-value correlations). The
